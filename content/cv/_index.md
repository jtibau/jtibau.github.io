## Experience

### Associate Professor of Computer Science
**Escuela Superior Politecnica del Litoral (ESPOL)** — Guayaquil, Ecuador | 2019–2024

Conducted HCI research using qualitative and mixed-methods approaches. Designed curricula for flipped-classroom courses in Programming (Python, Java, C++), Human-Computer Interaction (storyboards, wireframing, user stories, interviewing), and Game Design (ideation, game mechanics, playtesting).

Served as an elected member of the Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering Council (2020–2023), overseeing administrative and academic decisions across 5 undergraduate programs, 2 master's programs, and 2 doctoral programs. Provided guidance on the transition to online programs during the pandemic and the adoption of active learning methodologies.

### Ph.D. Candidate & Instructor
**Virginia Tech** — Blacksburg, Virginia | 2014–2019

Designed, built, and deployed FamilySong — a networked music-listening system using Raspberry Pi, NFC, Node.js, and Docker — across 12 households over 3 years. Built a spatial audio prototype for side-conversations in video calls, integrating a DoubleRobotics Duo Robot, Sennheiser Ambeo VR microphone, MaxMSP, and Audinate Dante.

Graduate Teaching Assistant and later Instructor of Record for "Computational Thinking," an introductory programming course for non-CS majors. Collaborated on course design, materials, and evaluation.

### Assistant Professor of Computer Science
**Escuela Superior Politecnica del Litoral (ESPOL)** — Guayaquil, Ecuador | 2011–2014

Scoped and managed a $200,000 budget to establish a Virtual and Augmented Reality laboratory, including a multi-projector power-wall, motion-capture environment, and AR equipment. The lab was later expanded in 2016 to include rapid prototyping and 3D printing.

Co-led ESPOL's Computer Science program to achieve Ecuador's first international ABET accreditation, which has been maintained since and supplemented with EUR-ACE accreditation in 2023.

### Research Assistant
**Universitat Politecnica de Catalunya** — Barcelona, Spain | 2010–2011

Developed C++/Qt/OpenGL middleware to provide VR/AR programmers with flexible deployment across CAVEs, HMDs, and other clustered multi-projector virtual environments.
